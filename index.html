Data and running instructions

Obtain the eight source workbooks from the official DREAM data page and place them in data/. Use DOWNLOAD_DATA.py to download and verify the files, then RUN_ALL.py to run steps 01–10 as described in the README. Outputs are ten Excel workbooks in results/.
